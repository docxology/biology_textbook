## Lab Context: Endocrine Signaling and Homeostasis {.unnumbered}

The endocrine system uses hormones (chemical messengers released into the bloodstream) to coordinate physiology across long timescales. Negative feedback loops maintain homeostasis: in the HPT axis, TRH (hypothalamus) → TSH (pituitary) → T3/T4 (thyroid) → inhibit TRH and TSH. In the innate immune system, Toll-like receptors (TLRs) detect conserved pathogen-associated molecular patterns (PAMPs; e.g., LPS, flagellin) and activate NF-κB, triggering pro-inflammatory cytokine production (IL-6, TNF-α, IL-1β).
