Calculation/mechanism reference: Q=HR×SV/1000 inL/min. Define absolute HR recovery as HRend−HRt and fractional excess-HR recovery as (HRend−HRt)/(HRend−HRrest). VC=VT+IRV+ERV and TLC=VC+RV; ordinary spirometry does not directly measure RV. Preload is filling-related myocardial stretch; Frank–Starling describes its effect on ejection, whereas contractility can change at the same preload. Raised plasma osmolality stimulates ADH, whose collecting-duct receptor signaling inserts AQP2 water channels; receptor loss can therefore coexist with high ADH and dilute urine. Hypoxia can increase ventilation rapidly and EPO-driven erythropoiesis more slowly. Ammonia requires abundant dilution water and little conversion cost; urea requires synthesis but reduces water demand; poorly soluble uric acid permits paste-like excretion at greater synthesis cost. These reference relationships do not diagnose the synthetic cases.

## Paper-Based Investigation {.unnumbered}

1. Calculate every cardiac output and plot both HR recovery curves. Compare absolute and fractional recovery; successive times in one person are not independent people.
2. Apply VO2=Q(CaO2−CvO2) forA at rest/exercise with Q inL/min and content in mLO2/Lblood. Explain why HR alone is insufficient. Distinguish preload-dependent Frank–Starling effects from contractility.
3. Calculate FEV1/FVC ratios and R vital/TLC volumes. Explain why low FVC without TLC or test quality does not establish restriction.
4. Compare ventilation and hematocrit timescales; propose hydration, EPO/reticulocyte and repeat-exposure controls. Hematocrit alone is not RBC mass.
5. Trace osmolality→ADH→AQP2;contrast receptor loss with hormone deficiency. Compare nitrogen excretion strategies. No medication recommendations.

## Data Recording {.unnumbered}

For each task record: given inputs with units; equation/assumption; intermediate arithmetic; result with units; replicate identifier; comparison/control; and one limitation. Draw graphs with labeled axes and retain individual observations rather than only a fitted line. Report range or sampleSD where replicates are supplied; do not invent confidence intervals from absent data.

## Paper-Based Evidence Upgrade {.unnumbered}

COPD oxygen responses involve V/Q redistribution and Haldane effects, not simply sustained loss of hypoxic drive. No breath holding, hyperventilation, exercise provocation or drug administration is required.

## Analysis and Submission {.unnumbered}

Submit labeled diagrams and calculations for every numbered investigation task, a concise results table or graph, and a claim–evidence–mechanism paragraph. Close with the strongest alternative explanation, a discriminating control, and a transfer decision whose assumptions are explicit. Clearly distinguish synthetic observations from cited biological mechanisms.

## Safety and Ethics {.unnumbered}

This is a paper-only exercise. Do not collect human/animal specimens, administer drugs, conduct stimulation or release organisms. Synthetic clinical-style patterns are not diagnoses or treatment advice. Respect source attribution, participant privacy, local rights and ecological uncertainty.
