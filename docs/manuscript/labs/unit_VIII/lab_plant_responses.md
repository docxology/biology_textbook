Mechanism reference: PIN proteins direct auxin transport. In shoots a lateral auxin excess can promote greater elongation on one side and bend growth; roots are more sensitive to auxin-mediated elongation inhibition. Auxin promotes removal of Aux/IAA transcriptional repressors. GA binding to GID1 promotes removal of DELLA growth repressors; a GA-insensitive line need not be rescued by adding GA. Red light converts phytochrome Pr toward Pfr, while far-red favors the reverse; Pfr promotes germination in this stipulated seed system. A causal hormone test compares a blocked pathway and matched rescue as well as hormone abundance.

## Paper-Based Investigation {.unnumbered}

1. Plot mean curvature and replicate range. For the 120 min root comparison compute mean difference between normal and starchless roots, and ask whether starch is required for full sensitivity or for every response.
2. Plot shoot and root elongation versus log10IAA, plotting zero as a separate control. Identify peak observed shoot growth; do not interpolate an exact physiological optimum from five doses. Explain root/shoot asymmetry and Aux/IAA degradation.
3. Calculate day0-to5 mean growth in the three GA groups. Distinguish GA-biosynthetic deficiency rescue from receptor/DELLA signaling insensitivity. Include solvent and genotype controls.
4. Calculate dish-wise germination fractions and means. Explain Pr→Pfr under red light and reversal under far-red. Identify terminal-light predictions and why dishes, not60 seeds, are the replicate count.
5. From the cards design one cue-specific stress or circadian test with timing, matched controls, and an alternative explanation. Connect a measured hormone change to evidence needed for causality.

## Data Recording {.unnumbered}

For each task record: given inputs with units; equation/assumption; intermediate arithmetic; result with units; replicate identifier; comparison/control; and one limitation. Draw graphs with labeled axes and retain individual observations rather than only a fitted line. Report range or sampleSD where replicates are supplied; do not invent confidence intervals from absent data.

## Paper-Based Evidence Upgrade {.unnumbered}

No ozone or climate trend is supplied: discuss UV-B only under a specified exposure change. A knockout phenotype requires matched background and rescue before assigning a unique gravity-sensing pathway.

## Analysis and Submission {.unnumbered}

Submit labeled diagrams and calculations for every numbered investigation task, a concise results table or graph, and a claim–evidence–mechanism paragraph. Close with the strongest alternative explanation, a discriminating control, and a transfer decision whose assumptions are explicit. Clearly distinguish synthetic observations from cited biological mechanisms.

## Safety and Ethics {.unnumbered}

This is a paper-only exercise. Do not collect human/animal specimens, administer drugs, conduct stimulation or release organisms. Synthetic clinical-style patterns are not diagnoses or treatment advice. Respect source attribution, participant privacy, local rights and ecological uncertainty.
