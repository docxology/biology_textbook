Life-cycle reference: pollen is the male gametophyte; the embryo sac inside an ovule is the female gametophyte. Fertilization joins egg+sperm to form the embryo and central cell+sperm to form endosperm; maternal integuments form the seed coat. Moss has a dominant haploid gametophyte bearing a dependent diploid sporophyte; fern has a dominant diploid sporophyte and a small haploid gametophyte. In both, motile sperm require a water film to reach an egg on the gametophyte. ABC identity rule: A alone→sepal, A+B→petal, B+C→stamen, C alone→carpel; in the simplified A-loss case C expands into the outer whorls. LURE–PRK6 mediates attraction toward the ovule; FER–LRE participates in reception and sperm release. These are distinct from germination and tube extension.

## Paper-Based Investigation {.unnumbered}

1. Label flower organs, seed tissue origins, and male/female gametophytes. Draw moss versus fern dominant generations and identify the water-dependent sperm stage.
2. Plot mean tube length against minutes for each treatment with between-drop sample SD at each time. Calculate 0–30 min and 90–120 min slopes within each drop, then their mean/sample SD in µm/min. Use each drop as an experimental unit; grains from one drop are subsamples.
3. Separate failure of germination, tube extension, self-incompatibility, ovular attraction (LURE–PRK6), and reception (FER–LRE). Design a compatible-pollen and temperature-matched control to locate failure.
4. Complete the three ploidy cases and ABC organ predictions. Explain why an endosperm dosage perturbation is not automatically a triploid embryo.
5. Design matched timed observations recording visits, conspecific pollen deposition, and seed set for two bee groups. Add a heat-only hand-pollination control to distinguish heat-damaged pollen from phenological mismatch. Evaluate whether ant movement benefits a seed.

## Data Recording {.unnumbered}

For each task record: given inputs with units; equation/assumption; intermediate arithmetic; result with units; replicate identifier; comparison/control; and one limitation. Draw graphs with labeled axes and retain individual observations rather than only a fitted line. Report range or sample SD where replicates are supplied; do not invent confidence intervals from absent data.

## Paper-Based Evidence Upgrade {.unnumbered}

The optional pollen model is saturating linear growth, $dL/dt=v(1-L/L_{\max})$, not logistic multiplication by $L$. Its exact solution is $L=L_{\max}-(L_{\max}-L_0)e^{-vt/L_{\max}}$. Use $L_0=0$, $L_{\max}=400$ µm and $v=4$ µm/min to predict $L(30\ \mathrm{min})\approx103.67$ µm. This illustrative model is not asserted to fit the supplied observations; its stated equation, not an apomixis experiment, defines the prediction.

## Analysis and Submission {.unnumbered}

Submit labeled diagrams and calculations for every numbered investigation task, a concise results table or graph, and a claim–evidence–mechanism paragraph. Close with the strongest alternative explanation, a discriminating control, and a transfer decision whose assumptions are explicit. Clearly distinguish synthetic observations from cited biological mechanisms.
