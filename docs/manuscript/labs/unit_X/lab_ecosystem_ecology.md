## Lab Context {.unnumbered}

All bottle, budget, nutrient and engineering observations are synthetic. The carbon packet is a deliberately closed teaching budget, not a named published annual estimate. Bottle mixtures include heterotrophs, so NCP is not NPP.

Nitrogen reference: diazotrophs are nitrogen-fixing bacteria/archaea whose nitrogenase requires energy and protection from O2; aerobic nitrifiers oxidize ammonium/nitrite. Plants and microbes assimilate inorganic N into organic matter. Decomposer microbes mineralize organic N; denitrifying microbes reduce nitrate under low-O2 conditions. For the budget use land NPP=GPP−plant respiration and net land sink=NPP−heterotroph respiration; ocean net sink=air-to-ocean minus ocean-to-air flux. Atmospheric accumulation equals the two anthropogenic inputs minus those net sinks. All five arrow types and every budget flux are printed here; adding net and gross versions of the same exchange would double-count it.

## Paper-Based Investigation {.unnumbered}

1. Compute NCP=(light−initial)/(2 h), R=(initial−dark)/(2 h) and GPP=NCP+R for each replicate. Convert to carbon with 12/32. Explain the equal-respiration assumption and why producer NPP requires a separate heterotrophic-respiration estimate.
2. Draw every gross carbon arrow once. Calculate NPP, land and ocean net sinks, and atmospheric accumulation. Never add net-sink arrows on top of their gross constituent fluxes.
3. Draw all five nitrogen transformations with organisms and conditions. Summarize chlorophyll/DO means and ranges. Design nutrient-only, shaded, aerated and fish-free controls to distinguish bloom, respiration and toxicity alternatives.
4. Calculate engineering-minus-control means for infiltration,N,C and turnover. A larger carbon stock need not imply slower turnover or net sequestration. Explain site-selection confounding and propose manipulative controls.
5. Compare nutrient-reduction or soil-management options using water quality, food production, biodiversity, costs and rights. Identify missing service evidence.

## Data Recording {.unnumbered}

For each task record: given inputs with units; equation/assumption; intermediate arithmetic; result with units; replicate identifier; comparison/control; and one limitation. Draw graphs with labeled axes and retain individual observations rather than only a fitted line. Report range or sample SD where replicates are supplied; do not invent confidence intervals from absent data.

## Paper-Based Evidence Upgrade {.unnumbered}

For Martin attenuation use $F(z)=F(z_0)(z/z_0)^{-b}$. With $z_0=100$ m, $b=1$ and $F(100\ \mathrm{m})=10$ mg C m−2 day−1, $F(1000\ \mathrm{m})=1$ mg C m−2 day−1. This is a power law, not an exponential half-depth or a sequestration-duration measurement.

## Analysis and Submission {.unnumbered}

Submit labeled diagrams and calculations for every numbered investigation task, a concise results table or graph, and a claim–evidence–mechanism paragraph. Close with the strongest alternative explanation, a discriminating control, and a transfer decision whose assumptions are explicit. Clearly distinguish synthetic observations from cited biological mechanisms.

## Safety and Ethics {.unnumbered}

This is a paper-only exercise. Do not collect human/animal specimens, administer drugs, conduct stimulation or release organisms. Synthetic clinical-style patterns are not diagnoses or treatment advice. Respect source attribution, participant privacy, local rights and ecological uncertainty.
