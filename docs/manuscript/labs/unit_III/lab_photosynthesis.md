# Lab — Photosynthesis {#sec:lab_unit_III_photosynthesis .unnumbered}

*This activity accompanies \cref{sec:unit_III_photosynthesis} of the textbook.*

## Learning Objectives {.unnumbered}

- Compare pigment absorption with whole-leaf light responses without equating them.
- Calculate photon energies with unit conversions.
- Relate oxygen evolution to linear electron flow and ATP/NADPH production.
- Calculate ATP supply relative to carbon-fixation demand and state limits of flotation as an efficiency proxy.

- **Chapter LO coverage:** LO2, LO3, LO6, LO13
- **Rubric (20 points total):**
  - **Evidence — 8 points (LO3, LO6, LO13):** Award 4 for 450/660 nm energies in J/photon and kJ/mol with nm→m and Avogadro conversions, 2 for dish1/three-dish ET50 values and declared reciprocal-of-mean rates with labeled plots, and 2 for the 12/(14/3)=18/7 ATP per 2 NADPH budget compared with 3:2 carbon-fixation demand. Preserve setup credit after arithmetic errors; do not treat photons per O2 as photons per CO2 without a stated coupling argument.
  - **Controls — 3 points (LO2, LO6):** Award 1 for matched incident photon flux, equal disc area and temperature in color comparisons, 1 for explaining the dark comparison's test of light dependence, and 1 for explaining the no-bicarbonate comparison's test of supplied carbon. Neither control by itself isolates all respiration or gas-retention effects.
  - **Uncertainty — 3 points (LO13):** Award 1 for using three independent dishes rather than ten discs or repeated times as n, 1 for reporting dark/no-bicarbonate ET50 as not reached by 20 min without extrapolating a rate, and 1 for naming how respiration, infiltration or gas retention can decouple flotation from carbon fixation. Identical synthetic intensity replicates are not evidence of perfect real precision.
  - **Mechanism — 4 points (LO2, LO6, LO13):** Award 2 for relating water-derived electron flow, NADPH production and lumenal protons to the supplied LEF budget, 1 for explaining how cyclic flow can supplement proton motive force/ATP without NADPH, and 1 for distinguishing pigment-extract absorbance from whole-leaf light capture and action. A low green extract value does not establish zero green photochemistry.
  - **Transfer — 2 points (LO2, LO13):** Award 1 for explaining why the inverse-square reciprocal-ET50 prediction may fail with an extended LED source or saturation, and 1 for a canopy interpretation that considers penetration/acclimation and proposes paired net CO2/O2 exchange to test whether a flotation ranking represents carbon-fixation efficiency. A claim that all green light is useless earns no canopy point.

## Paper-Based Investigation {.unnumbered}

1. Determine dish1 ET50 and compute three-dish means and min–max ranges for each color. Use $1/\overline{ET50}$ as the declared descriptive rate convention, not the mean of the three individual reciprocals. For dark/no-bicarbonate dish1 controls, report ET50 not reached by 20 min; do not assign a zero time or extrapolated rate.

2. Plot1/ET50 versus relative inverse-square flux; identify which assumption would fail for a saturating leaf or an extended LED panel.

3. Use $E=hc/\lambda$, with 1 nm=10⁻⁹ m, to calculate J per photon and kJ per mole at 450 and 660 nm. Explain why green absorption in extracted pigments does not establish that green photons are useless to a canopy.

4. Account for ATP/NADPH per O2 under the supplied LEF/c14 assumptions; compare with 3 ATP:2 NADPH demand per CO2. Interpret the C3/CAM gas cards and distinguish a named 2100 scenario from a forecast.

## Data Recording {.unnumbered}

Record each calculation with units, the comparison/control, and one alternative explanation. Show intermediate steps; use a separate column for observed card evidence versus inferred mechanism.

## Paper-Based Evidence Upgrade {.unnumbered}

Flotation integrates oxygen production, oxygen use, gas retention, tissue anatomy and infiltration, not a direct linear carbon-fixation assay. The ten discs within one dish are subsamples; dishes supply n=3. No ANOVA on timepoints or claims of clinical significance. A matched photon-flux control is essential for color comparisons; canopy penetration and spectral acclimation limit extract-to-plant transfer.

## Analysis Questions {.unnumbered}

Explain which result supports the proposed mechanism, which result would falsify it, and what additional measurement would discriminate the strongest alternative. Apply the mechanism to a new tissue, genotype, or condition without treating synthetic observations as clinical evidence.

> **Concept Check:** Why can two dishes have the same ET50 despite different carbon-fixation rates, and which paired gas-exchange measurement would discriminate them?
