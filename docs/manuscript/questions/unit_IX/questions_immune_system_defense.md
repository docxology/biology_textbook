2. What distinguishes a pattern-recognition receptor from an antibody receptor?

<!-- SOLUTION
**Answer (Q2, Recall).** PRRs recognize recurring microbial/damage patterns using germline-encoded specificity. B-cell receptors are clonally diversified and bind particular antigenic structures; both can initiate signaling but have different recognition repertoires.

**Scoring guide (4 points).** 2 points: pattern-versus-clonal recognition; 2 points: distinct origin of diversity. Award partial credit for correct intermediate reasoning; for quantitative work require a stated unit/boundary when applicable.

3. Name the principal complement fragments for opsonization, inflammatory recruitment, and membrane attack.

<!-- SOLUTION

**Scoring guide (4 points).** 2 points: correct fragments; 2 points: distinct outcome of terminal defect. Award partial credit for correct intermediate reasoning; for quantitative work require a stated unit/boundary when applicable.

4. Contrast MHC-I and MHC-II presentation.

<!-- SOLUTION
**Answer (Q4, Recall).** MHC-I presents predominantly cytosolic peptides to CD8 T cells; MHC-II presents predominantly endosomal peptides to CD4 cells on professional APCs. Cross-presentation is an important exception, not proof that the usual compartments are identical.

**Scoring guide (4 points).** 2 points: compartment and T-cell pairing; 2 points: cross-presentation caveat. Award partial credit for correct intermediate reasoning; for quantitative work require a stated unit/boundary when applicable.

<!-- SOLUTION

**Scoring guide (4 points).** 2 points: variable-region selection; 2 points: constant-region replacement. Award partial credit for correct intermediate reasoning; for quantitative work require a stated unit/boundary when applicable.

8. Why can a conjugate vaccine produce a different response from unconjugated polysaccharide?

<!-- SOLUTION
**Answer (Q8, Recall).** A polysaccharide-specific B cell internalizes linked carrier protein and presents its peptides to helper T cells. This recruits T-dependent germinal-center help, memory, and class switching; the carrier is not merely an extra innate adjuvant.

**Scoring guide (4 points).** 2 points: linked recognition and presentation; 2 points: T-dependent memory. Award partial credit for correct intermediate reasoning; for quantitative work require a stated unit/boundary when applicable.

9. Name measurable readouts for phagocytosis, complement killing, and adaptive memory.

<!-- SOLUTION

**Scoring guide (4 points).** 2 points: three appropriate readouts; 2 points: mechanism-specific controls. Award partial credit for correct intermediate reasoning; for quantitative work require a stated unit/boundary when applicable.

12. Synthetic active-serum survivors are 80/100 wild-type meningococci and 20/100 fHbp-deletion cells; heat-inactivated serum gives 98/100 for both. Interpret.

<!-- SOLUTION
**Answer (Q12, Application).** Active-serum survival falls from 80% to 20% with deletion, a 60-percentage-point difference, while heated-serum survival is 98% for both. **Scoring guide (4 points).** 2 points: 60-percentage-point survival difference; 2 points: complement/complementation controls. Award partial credit for correct intermediate reasoning; for quantitative work require a stated unit/boundary when applicable.

17. Under a homogeneous transmission model R0=4 and a perfectly infection-blocking immune fraction p, derive the threshold.

<!-- SOLUTION
**Answer (Q17, Application).** $R=4(1-p)$, so $R<1$ requires $p>0.75$ ($p=0.75$ gives $R=1$). A disease-only vaccine effect cannot be substituted for infection/transmission protection in this formula.

**Scoring guide (4 points).** 2 points: 0.75 with strict inequality; 2 points: correct transmission endpoint. Award partial credit for correct intermediate reasoning; for quantitative work require a stated unit/boundary when applicable.
SOLUTION -->
