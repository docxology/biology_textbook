## Review Defaults

- Preserve the config-driven chapter, lab, and question-bank roster; additions require curriculum review.
- Add embedded improvements only; do not add new renderable chapter surfaces.
- Cite or qualify recent and numeric claims.
- Keep required labs paper-based; optional material extensions stay clearly optional.
- Use `\cref{...}` for numbered targets and `\nameref{...}` for unnumbered targets; retain semantic figure/equation labels instead of hard-coded rendered numbers.
